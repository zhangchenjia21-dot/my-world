# MW-012 Owner-approved Character Content Input — 张琛 v0.1

Status: **OWNER-APPROVED CONTENT INPUT**  
Purpose: source content for a Player Character Card selectable during New Game  
Owner-provided: 2026-09-05  
Target world family: `world.han_end.unsettled_realm` / 汉末三国  
Target role: Player Character

## 1. Identity

- Name: **张琛**
- Age: **24**
- Sex: **male**
- Origin: modern China
- Arrival premise: **physical body transport** into the selected Three Kingdoms Entry/T0 scene. He arrives as himself, from zero, with no pre-existing local identity, household, patron, official status, faction membership or acquaintances.
- Background: retired People's Armed Police conscript; 985-university graduate; history enthusiast.

## 2. Core personality

- steady / composed;
- rational;
- fair-minded;
- decisive.

He tends to observe and reason before acting, but once a decision is necessary he does not habitually procrastinate. He values order, responsibility and fairness and dislikes empty posturing.

## 3. Initial goals

Primary initial goals:

1. survive and establish basic security;
2. seek a possible route home.

Longer-term direction is intentionally **not predetermined**. Depending on lived events and Player choice, he may:

- assist a ruler/leader he judges worthy;
- remain independent;
- build his own organization or political/military power.

No card text may pre-author which route he chooses.

## 4. Moral boundaries

- does not slaughter innocents;
- does not lightly betray trust or sworn commitments;
- does not proactively harm common people;
- does not commit grave wrongdoing purely for private gain.

These are strong character values, not a machine prohibition layer. The Player still owns meaningful protagonist choices.

## 5. Physical profile

- strong, fit physique;
- good stamina;
- visible military bearing;
- modern close-combat training / fighting fundamentals.

Do not inflate this into superhuman combat ability, ancient battlefield mastery, weapon mastery he has never learned, or immunity to injury.

## 6. Knowledge and intellectual strengths

- broad modern university-level foundation in economics, philosophy, sociology and history;
- strong interest in institutions, historical development, political order, social structure and war;
- capable of analyzing logistics, organization, incentives, governance and structural causes rather than viewing war only as battlefield tactics;
- almost able to recite the major Three Kingdoms event line from memory;
- knows the names/backgrounds of most figures who became notable in *Romance of the Three Kingdoms* / common Three Kingdoms historical culture.

### Historical-knowledge authority boundary

His remembered history is **character memory / belief from his original world**, not current Game truth and not guaranteed future canon.

Therefore:

```text
Zhang Chen remembers historical event X
!= X must happen in this Game
!= NPC Y must follow the remembered destiny
!= GM/world evolution must preserve the source timeline
```

Once the Game diverges, his historical knowledge may become incomplete, misleading or obsolete. He may use it as a hypothesis/advantage, never as omniscient runtime authority.

Knowing a historical person's name/biography also does **not** automatically mean he can visually identify an unknown stranger without sufficient in-world clues.

## 7. Limitations

- social ability is below average relative to his analytical strengths;
- strongly dislikes bureaucratic smoothness, corruption and cynical official conduct, which can create interpersonal/political friction;
- arrives with no local network, credentials, property, clan protection or faction backing;
- has not learned clerical script (隶书) and initially cannot read Three Kingdoms-era written material; literacy may be learned through play;
- modern knowledge does not automatically grant ancient craft skill, engineering implementation ability, command legitimacy, bureaucratic fluency or unlimited technological reproduction.

## 8. Initial carried items

He physically arrives with only:

- military-style water bottle;
- military utility/combat knife;
- wristwatch;
- compass;
- portable compressed food.

These are finite physical possessions. They do not imply modern resupply, ammunition, communications, electricity, databases or other equipment not explicitly listed.

## 9. Relationship / social starting state

No person in the Three Kingdoms era knows Zhang Chen at T0.

He has no automatic relationship, reputation, official identity, faction allegiance or trust bond.

His historical knowledge means he knows *about* many famous figures, but they do not know him and owe him nothing.

## 10. T0 compatibility intent

The card should be selectable as Player Character for the currently supported Han-end/Three-Kingdoms Entries. For whichever Entry the Player selects, the arrival premise is:

> Zhang Chen's physical body is transported into **that Entry's opening present**, with his modern memory and listed possessions intact, but with no prior local life history.

Compatibility should cover the currently supported Entries unless a fresh schema audit proves an explicit incompatibility:

- 184 Yellow Turban start;
- 189 Luoyang crisis;
- 196 Emperor Xu / Xuchang-era start;
- 200 Guandu eve;
- 208 Red Cliffs eve;
- 214 Yizhou transition;
- 220 Han–Wei transition.

His age remains 24 at whichever selected T0; this is a game-start alternative, not one continuous cross-entry biography.

## 11. Player-agency boundary

The card may establish traits, memories, values, initial possessions and starting circumstances. It must **not** pre-author future meaningful protagonist decisions such as choosing Liu Bei/Cao Cao/Sun Quan, swearing allegiance, founding a faction, revealing future knowledge, killing/sparing someone, or deciding to stay in the past.

GM may advance consequences, NPCs and the world; new meaningful Zhang Chen choices remain with the Player.

## 12. Non-scope for v0.1

- portrait/image generation;
- numeric RPG stats or fixed HP/STR/INT scores;
- new mechanic Expansion;
- new Character Card schema;
- new language-simulation system;
- guaranteed future history;
- automatic famous-person recognition without in-world evidence;
- UI redesign.
