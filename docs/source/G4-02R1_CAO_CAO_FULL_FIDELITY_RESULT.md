# G4-02R1｜曹操 Full-Fidelity Character Result

## Verdict

> **PASS at semantic/content candidate level.**

The candidate covers all six valid Han Entries and preserves the original Character Card's major semantic categories while making mature organization/control semantics depend on lived history.

This is not loader/runtime implementation PASS and not final G4-02R1 PASS.

## Original semantic coverage

Original major sections remain represented across exact T0 profiles:

- identity anchor → thin top-level continuity + current T0 identity;
- personality / contradictions → evidence-backed current tensions, not one full-life adult template;
- capabilities / limitations → current earned scale only;
- decision / behavior logic → current organization scale and actual pressures;
- relationships / autonomy → ability, results, trust, political role and independent actor interests remain explicit;
- expression → concise/executive style plus private variability, without `奸雄腔` stereotype;
- knowledge / information boundary → current networks remain fallible; no external future authority;
- T0/history boundary → exact bindings + open future.

The migration does not reduce Cao Cao to `summary + traits + drives`.

## Conditional-personality fidelity

Three original nuances are explicitly preserved:

1. **talent inclusion ↔ control risk** — capable/heterogeneous people can receive room, but not unlimited political safety;
2. **pragmatic tolerance ↔ security severity** — tolerance depends on current value/risk, not a fixed merciful/cruel label;
3. **challenge != automatic hostility** — disagreement is judged through evidence, execution value, public authority and loss-of-control risk.

These structures become richer only after the T0 has enough organization/political experience to support them.

## Relationship / expression / knowledge

The candidate keeps:

- private affection vs political role as distinct but interacting layers;
- trust based on repeated competence/results/reliability rather than player privilege;
- subordinates and rivals as autonomous actors rather than tools;
- public language that can be concise, political and literary without fixed villain performance;
- large information networks that remain subject to delay, lies, faction filters and local unknowns.

## Early individuality

184 remains a specific young Cao Cao through:

- high-official family ecology;
- early formal office and enforcement experience;
- recorded cleverness / tactical calculation;
- youthful renxia and higher willingness to take unconventional action;
- existing tension between formal rules and actual execution.

This remains distinguishable from 184 Liu Bei without relying on future faction/state identity.

## Closed coverage

Bindings exist only for:

`184 / 189 / 196 / 200 / 208 / 214`.

Within `world.han_end.unsettled_realm`, 220 and later Entries are therefore temporally incompatible. There is no identity-only resurrection fallback.
