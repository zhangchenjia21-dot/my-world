## 2. Disclosure audit

`gm_reference` does **not** mean player-known.

`gm_private` is reserved for explicitly authored backstage truth, not merely “something a normal resident may not know”.

Examples:

```text
public/reference:
Great Fracture happened;
Viator no longer appears as a normal complete god;
constructed-person personhood is socially disputed;
Ascension-failure theory exists as one public explanation.

private:
Viator's exact fragmentation role/state;
independent-soul-boundary failure inside the Ascension Network;
which gods knew/supported/opposed which parts;
at least some constructed persons demonstrably possess full souls;
modern research has again approached critical ancient mechanisms.
```

No Character gains those private answers automatically.

莉维娅 explicitly lacks the complete Great Fracture truth despite elite academic ability.
阿德里安 lacks state/anti-magic/Great-Fracture secrets despite noble/professional status.
杜恩 lacks sealed-ruin and Viator truth despite long field experience.

Therefore:

> **World Truth != Character Knowledge != Player Knowledge** survives migration.

## 7. World / Character ownership audit

World owns:

- magic/metaphysics;
- gods;
- nations/institutions;
- public/secret Great Fracture facts;
- social/legal structures;
- World trends and geography.

Character owns:

- that person's personality and stable starting capabilities;
- what they personally know and why;
- their private pre-T0 history;
- their pre-T0 relationship hooks;
- their decision/expression patterns.

Character does not duplicate the complete World truth.

World does not duplicate full Character personality.

Past relationship hooks do not become a current relationship state.
